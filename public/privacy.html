Privacy policy

Last updated 26 September 2026 · applies to Hot Tail on this website

Hot Tail is a free game with no ads, no accounts, no cookies and no third-party trackers. This page lists everything the game stores or sends, and how to switch it off or delete it.

On your device

The game keeps your settings, key bindings, best scores, practice progress and your best stage-1 run (for the title-screen demo) in your browser's local storage. It also creates a random player ID (24 hex characters, not linked to you) and the callsign you type. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes all of it. An offline copy of the game is cached by a service worker.

When you post a high score

Submitting a score sends: your random player ID, callsign, score, stage reached, mode, jet, difficulty, game version and a recording of your controller inputs for that run (used to verify the score by replaying it). Scores and callsigns appear publicly on the leaderboard. To limit abuse, the server stores a salted one-way hash of your IP address with each score; the IP address itself is never stored.

Delete your scores any time: Settings → Gameplay → Delete my leaderboard scores. This removes every score tied to your player ID, locally and on the server.

Anonymous statistics and error reports

Unless you turn it off (Settings → Gameplay → Anonymous stats & error reports), the game sends daily counters such as "stage 4 was cleared" or "a player died on stage 7", how long loading took (rounded to 250 ms), and technical error messages (error text, code location, game version and browser name). These carry no player ID, callsign or IP address, and only aggregates are kept.

Who processes the data

The website and API are hosted by Vercel, and leaderboard data is stored in a Supabase Postgres database. Like any web host, they process standard request logs to deliver the service. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

Retention

Leaderboard entries are kept until you delete them or the leaderboard is reset. Aggregate statistics and error reports are kept for up to 12 months.

Children

The game collects no personal information knowingly from anyone; callsigns are filtered for offensive words.
