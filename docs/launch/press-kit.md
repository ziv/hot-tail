# Hot Tail — press kit

**Hot Tail** is a free arcade jet-combat game that runs in any modern browser — desktop or phone — with no ads and no download.

## Description

Strap into a jet and tear across 18 short, loud stages — open ocean, a desert canyon, glacier passes, a neon city at night and the edge of space. Sweep your reticle across up to eight targets and let a missile volley fly, barrel-roll through incoming fire, then go up against four giant bosses: a flying fortress, a carrier group, a cloaking stealth ace and an orbital platform. A full run takes about 25 minutes.

## Features

- 18 stages, 5 worlds, 4 multi-phase bosses, 12 enemy types, 3 jets
- Lock-on missile volleys, guns, flares, rolls, boost and air-brake
- Arcade, weekly Score Attack and Practice modes, with leaderboards where every run is replayed on the server to verify it
- Modern 3D or a retro "super-scaler" sprite look
- Keyboard, mouse, gamepad and touch; full rebinding; colour-blind HUD palettes, aim assist and more
- Runs at 60 fps on a mid-range laptop; about 200 KB to download (everything — art, music, sound — is generated in code)
