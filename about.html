HOTTAIL

Arcade jet combat · free in your browser · no ads

PLAY NOW WATCH TRAILER

18 stages, 5 worlds

Open ocean, a desert canyon run, glacier valleys, a neon city at night and the edge of space.

Lock on. Let fly.

Sweep the reticle over up to eight targets and release a missile volley. Guns for air, missiles for everything on the ground.

4 giant bosses

A flying fortress, a carrier group, a cloaking stealth ace and an orbital platform — each with three phases.

Roll, boost, brake

Barrel-roll through missiles, burn past tail-chasers or brake hard to make them overshoot.

Modern or retro

Switch between modern 3D and a super-scaler sprite look, rendered from the same game.

Fair leaderboards

Every submitted run is replayed on the server before it counts — no fake scores.

Controls

Steer WASD / arrows · mouse · left stick · touch stick
Guns Space / J · left mouse · A · AUTO on touch
Lock & missiles Hold K / right mouse / X, release to fire
Barrel roll L · double-tap a direction · B
Boost / brake Shift / Q · RT / LT
Flares R · Y

Keyboard, mouse, gamepad and touch. Keys can be rebound; aim assist, toggle controls and colour-blind HUD palettes are in Settings.
